Concept sheet of a stylized videogame prop: a wooden TREASURE CHEST. A rectangular wooden box with a half-round arched lid on top; three dark iron straps run vertically over the front of the box, over the arched lid and down the back; a gold lock plate sits in the middle of the front, just under the lid, with a small black keyhole. Warm brown wood planks, dark grey iron straps, gold lock plate.

Three orthographic views on one pure white background, arranged left to right with the SAME scale and standing on one shared thin grey ground line: (1) FRONT elevation, (2) LEFT SIDE elevation (the arch of the lid seen as a half circle), (3) TOP-DOWN plan view placed to the right. Leave wide white gaps between the views. Flat cel-shaded colours with thin clean black ink outlines, no gradients, no shadows, no perspective, no text.
